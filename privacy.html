Privacy Policy

NaviList, the Netherlands · navilistapp@gmail.com · Last updated 27 October 2026

1. Who we are

The controller of your personal data in the NaviList app is NaviList, the Netherlands. Write to navilistapp@gmail.com for anything about your data.

This policy covers the NaviList app. The App Store and Google Play handle your store account and your payments under their own policies.

2. The short version

  • NaviList works without an account. Then everything you enter stays on your device.
  • If you create an account, your lists and store cards are stored on our servers, so they can sync between your devices and be shared with the people you invite.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking tools in the app, and we never sell your data.

3. What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, and the display name you enter.
  • Your content: the lists you make and their titles, the items, quantities and notes you add, the categories and shops you use, and your saved store cards (the shop and the card number).
  • Sharing: the email addresses you invite, and who is a member of a shared account.
  • Purchases: which plan was bought and whether it is active. The payment itself — your card or bank details — is handled by Apple or Google and their payment provider, and we never see it.
  • Automatic sorting: items the app cannot place on its own, together with the names of your categories, are sent to our server, which asks an AI service to choose a category. This only happens while you are online. A word may also be added to a shared list of item names and how often they are used, with no link to you, so that sorting works better for everyone.
  • Camera and photos: the app uses the camera, or a photo you choose, to read the barcode of a store card. The picture is read on your device, is not stored, and is not uploaded.
  • App updates: each time you open the app it asks Expo, the service that builds and delivers NaviList, whether a newer version is available. That request carries your IP address, the app version and the platform, and nothing about you or your lists.

We do not collect your location, your contacts, or an advertising identifier. There is no advertising or analytics software in the app.

4. Why we use it

  • To run the app and keep your lists in sync — because you asked us to, as part of our agreement with you.
  • To let the people you invite see and edit a shared account — which is what inviting them means.
  • To process your NaviList Pro purchase, and to meet our tax and bookkeeping duties.
  • To keep the service secure and free of abuse, and to answer your questions — our legitimate interest in running the app properly.
  • To send you a password-reset email when you ask for one, or an account-related email when it is necessary.

5. Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers to run NaviList:

  • Supabase — hosting, sign-in, the database, and the function that sorts items.
  • Expo (EAS) — builds the app, and answers the update check it makes on launch.
  • RevenueCat — keeping track of what has been purchased and what is unlocked.
  • Apple and Google — the stores that distribute the app and take payment.
  • An AI provider that classifies item text for the automatic sorting described above.
  • The people you invite into a shared account — the lists, items and cards in that account.

The AI service is used on a free tier: it may therefore also use the item words and category names it is sent — never your name, your email address, or anything that identifies you or your account — to improve its own products. Apart from that, these providers may only use your data to supply their service to us. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for advertising.

Some of these providers are outside the European Economic Area. Where that is the case, the transfer is covered by appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

6. How long we keep it

  • On your device: until you delete the item, or delete the app.
  • On our servers, if you have an account: until you delete the content in the app, or delete your account. Deleted content is removed from the database and falls out of our backups as they roll over.
  • Purchase records: for as long as tax and accounting rules require.
  • Invitations: until they are accepted, cancelled, or removed with the account.

You can delete your account and everything in it yourself, at any time, in Settings → Account. That removes your lists, items, saved cards and shops from our database, and takes your email address off any invitation that is still waiting. It cannot be undone. If you cannot get into the app, write to navilistapp@gmail.com and we will do it for you.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to see your data, correct it, delete it, receive a copy in a portable form, and restrict or object to how it is used. Write to navilistapp@gmail.com and we will answer within one month.

You can also complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, or to the data protection authority in your own country.

8. Security

Your data is encrypted while it travels between the app and our servers, and a shared account is reachable only by its members. No system is perfect, so please use a password you do not use anywhere else.

9. Children

NaviList is not intended for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has given us their data, write to navilistapp@gmail.com and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes we update the date at the top, and we point a significant change out in the app.
