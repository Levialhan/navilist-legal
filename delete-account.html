Deleting your account and data

NaviList, the Netherlands · navilistapp@gmail.com · Last updated 27 October 2026

1. This page

NaviList is a shopping list app made by NaviList, the Netherlands. This page explains how to have your NaviList account and the data in it deleted, and what is deleted and kept when you do. It covers the NaviList app for Android, as listed on Google Play.

You can delete everything yourself, in the app, in a few seconds — you do not need to write to us, and there is no waiting period. If you cannot get into the app, write to us instead. Both ways are below.

2. Delete it yourself, in the app

  • Open NaviList.
  • Tap the menu button at the top left of your lists screen.
  • Open the Account tab and choose "Delete account".
  • Confirm. Your account and everything in it are deleted immediately, and you are signed out. This cannot be undone.

If you bought NaviList Pro, that is a separate step. A subscription keeps renewing until you cancel it in Google Play (Play Store → your profile picture → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions). Deleting your account does not cancel the subscription, and it does not refund the period you have already paid for.

3. If you cannot get into the app

Write to navilistapp@gmail.com and say that you want your NaviList account deleted. Send it from the email address on the account if you can; if you cannot, tell us that address so that we can find the right account. You do not need to reinstall the app, and you do not need an account to write to us.

We will delete the account and everything in it within 30 days, and write back to tell you it is done.

4. What is deleted

  • Your account, and the email address on it.
  • Your display name.
  • Every list in the account, with its items, quantities, notes, categories and shops.
  • Every saved store card.
  • Invitations that nobody has accepted yet, and your place in a shared account. If other people were invited to your lists, they lose access to them.

5. What is kept, and for how long

  • Purchase records. Your payment details are held by Google, not by us. We keep only that a purchase was made and whether it is still active, for as long as tax and bookkeeping rules require — up to seven years.
  • Backups. Deleted content leaves our live database at once. Our backups roll over, so a copy can survive for a short time; it is gone within 30 days.
  • The shared list of item names used to sort items automatically. That list carries no link to you or to your account, so it cannot be deleted on request — and nothing else about you is in it.

Nothing else is kept. We do not keep a copy of your lists, items or store cards after your account is deleted.

6. Questions and complaints

Write to navilistapp@gmail.com and we will answer within one month. You can also complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, or to the data protection authority in your own country.
